About Me

I am a passionate Medical Engineer with a strong focus on Software Development. My unique combination of technical expertise and hands-on healthcare experience allows me to bridge the gap between cutting-edge technology and practical medical applications.

I hold a BSc and MSc in Medical Engineering from KTH Royal Institute of Technology, along with a BSc in Nursing. My expertise includes software engineering, data analysis, algorithm development, and advanced medical imaging systems such as MRI, ultrasound, and ionizing radiation.

My clinical background in healthcare provides me with a valuable perspective on the real-world needs of healthcare professionals and patients, enabling me to approach challenges with a holistic, user-centered, and innovative mindset.

Driven, detail-oriented, and eager to learn, I am committed to creating impactful and sustainable solutions that advance medical technology. My ambition is to continue refining my skills as a software developer and contribute to transformative innovations in healthcare.
